Kwokka privacy policy

Kwokka is a private app used only by its owner and their family. It is not offered to anyone else.

What Kwokka accesses

With each user's permission, Kwokka has read-only access to that user's Gmail and Google Calendar. It cannot send, change or delete email, or create or change calendar events.

How it is used

Kwokka reads email and calendar events only when the user asks it a question that needs them. To answer, it sends the parts it reads to Anthropic's Claude service, which processes them and returns an answer. Nothing is sold, shared for advertising, or used for any other purpose.

Where it is kept

Kwokka runs on the owner's own computer. The Google sign-in is stored there and nowhere else. Email and calendar contents are not kept after a question is answered.

Removing access

A user can disconnect in Kwokka at any time, or remove access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
